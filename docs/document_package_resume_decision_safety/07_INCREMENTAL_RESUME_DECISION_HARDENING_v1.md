# 07 INCREMENTAL RESUME DECISION HARDENING v1

Status: active_canonical
Document Type: canonical
Authority Level: operational
Interpretation Priority: high
Scope: incremental hardening rule for decision resumption across the documentation universe
Rule: resume-decision hardening must remain incremental so trusted continuation of package decision flow improves without causing retrofit chaos

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the incremental-resume-decision-hardening rule of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- practical decision hardening
- lower retrofit overload
- continuity between decision ambition and real maintenance work
- a stable base for later tooling

---

## 2. Hardening Principle

Incremental resume decision hardening should remain understandable in terms of:
- what package family is hardened next
- why it is prioritized
- what minimum decision-safety quality is expected
- how hardening grows without chaos

---

## 3. Required Rule

Incremental resume decision hardening should remain:
- explicit
- incremental
- canonical-first
- practical
- non-chaotic

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- pretending full decision hardening will happen instantly
- uncontrolled retrofit pressure
- random hardening order
- decision hardening with no readable sequencing

---

## 5. Final Rule

A mature documentation system hardens decision resumption in a controlled sequence, not through one blind sweep.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical incremental-resume-decision-hardening rule until replaced by a stricter hardening reference.
