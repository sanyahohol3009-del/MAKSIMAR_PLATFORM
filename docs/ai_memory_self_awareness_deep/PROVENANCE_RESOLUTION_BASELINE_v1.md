# PROVENANCE RESOLUTION BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical provenance-resolution baseline
Scope: how the platform should resolve source origin, authority level, and continuity meaning for remembered material
Rule: provenance resolution must remain explicit so memory, documentation, operator input, and inferred continuity do not collapse into one undifferentiated authority surface

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the provenance-resolution baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- readable source-origin interpretation
- bounded distinction among remembered materials
- explicit relation between provenance and authority
- a stable base for later provenance conflict and trust-resolution families

---

## 2. Provenance Principle

Provenance should remain understandable in terms of:
- where a memory or continuity artifact came from
- what kind of source produced it
- what authority level it may carry
- what it may support without replacing canonical truth

Not all remembered or retrieved material is equal.

---

## 3. Resolution Intent

Provenance resolution should help answer:
- whether a source is canonical, operator-provided, inferred, documented, or runtime-derived
- what confidence may reasonably attach to it
- what it may override
- what it must never silently replace

---

## 4. Required Rule

Provenance-resolution thinking should remain:
- explicit
- source-aware
- bounded
- authority-aware
- consistent with memory, governance, and documentation baselines

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- memory with no readable source origin
- inferred continuity treated as automatically canonical
- provenance ignored in favor of convenient certainty
- authority inflation through vague recall language

---

## 6. Final Rule

A mature AI platform resolves provenance before it claims continuity with confidence.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical provenance-resolution baseline until replaced by a stricter provenance and trust-resolution reference.
