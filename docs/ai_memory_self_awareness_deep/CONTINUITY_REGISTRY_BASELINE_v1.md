# CONTINUITY REGISTRY BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical continuity-registry baseline
Scope: registry-backed handling of persistent continuity entities across sessions and project phases
Rule: continuity should become registry-backed over time so important memory entities remain nameable, traceable, and governable

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the continuity-registry baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- stable naming of important continuity entities
- traceable relation between memory and source material
- bounded continuity tracking across sessions and phases
- a stable base for later registry-backed continuity families

---

## 2. Registry Principle

A continuity registry should remain understandable in terms of:
- what continuity entity exists
- what it refers to
- what source class it depends on
- what authority and confidence it carries
- how it may evolve without becoming unbounded myth

Registry-backed continuity is more disciplined than loose recall alone.

---

## 3. Registry Intent

The continuity registry should help answer:
- what important continuity objects the platform tracks
- how they remain identifiable over time
- what provenance they depend on
- how continuity can stay readable rather than chat-fragmented

---

## 4. Required Rule

Continuity-registry thinking should remain:
- explicit
- traceable
- provenance-aware
- bounded
- consistent with governance and long-term memory discipline

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- continuity entities with no stable identity
- registry-free continuity sprawl
- remembered structure that cannot be traced back to source classes
- continuity inflation through vague persistent labels

---

## 6. Final Rule

A mature AI platform benefits from continuity that is registry-backed, not only vaguely remembered.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical continuity-registry baseline until replaced by a stricter continuity-registry reference.
