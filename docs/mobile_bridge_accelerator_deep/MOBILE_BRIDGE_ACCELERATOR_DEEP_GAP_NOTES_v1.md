## 1. Purpose

This document records the current limitations of the mobile/bridge/accelerator deep pass.

It exists to keep the pass honest and bounded.

---

## 2. Current Gap Notes

### Gap 1
The current pass establishes a deeper family baseline, not yet a full concrete bridge-interface or per-transport implementation reference.

### Gap 2
The pass does not yet replace detailed handshake-flow procedures, transport-specific runbooks, or device-specific capability matrices.

### Gap 3
Per-backend routing decisions and per-device policy mappings are still future work.

### Gap 4
Future deeper documentation is still needed for:
- concrete bridge contracts
- discovery and handshake flow procedures
- transport-specific references
- backend-selection policy details
- thermal and power procedure families
- tighter linkage to app-shell and device-role documentation

---

## 3. Required Rule

These notes do not invalidate the baseline.
They show where later hardening is still needed.

---

## 4. Final Rule

A mobile/bridge/accelerator deep baseline may begin at family level if its incompleteness is explicit.
