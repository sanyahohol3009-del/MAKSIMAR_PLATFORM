# THERMAL AND POWER POLICY BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical thermal/power policy baseline
Scope: thermal and power-aware control of mobile and external accelerator behavior
Rule: thermal and power policy must remain explicit so capability reduction and safety-preserving fallback are governed rather than accidental

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the thermal and power policy baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- readable thermal-awareness
- bounded power-aware behavior
- controlled degradation under constrained mobile conditions
- a stable base for later thermal and power procedure families

---

## 2. Thermal Principle

Thermal state should remain understandable in terms of:
- current operating pressure
- effect on sustained capability
- relationship to fallback and degraded modes
- need for bounded reduction rather than uncontrolled instability

Thermal policy is not optional polish.
It is operational legitimacy.

---

## 3. Power Principle

Power state should remain understandable in terms of:
- battery or external power condition
- effect on mode selection
- effect on transport or execution continuity
- justification for reduced capability when needed

Power policy is not merely battery convenience.
It is part of safe continuity.

---

## 4. Required Rule

Thermal and power policy thinking should remain:
- explicit
- bounded
- continuity-aware
- safety-preserving
- consistent with fallback and backend-routing logic

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- thermal behavior treated as invisible until collapse
- power-aware decisions left to undocumented habit
- uncontrolled degradation under constrained mobile conditions
- performance preference overriding continuity and safety discipline

---

## 6. Final Rule

A mature mobile system respects thermal and power reality instead of pretending ideal conditions always exist.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical thermal/power policy baseline until replaced by a stricter mobile operating policy reference.
