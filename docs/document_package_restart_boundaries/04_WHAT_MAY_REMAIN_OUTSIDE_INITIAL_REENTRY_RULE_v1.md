# 04 WHAT MAY REMAIN OUTSIDE INITIAL REENTRY RULE v1

Status: active_canonical
Document Type: canonical
Authority Level: operational
Interpretation Priority: high
Scope: rule for what may remain outside the initial package restart frame
Rule: non-essential package material may remain outside the initial reentry frame so restart stays usable and does not collapse under avoidable overload

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the what-may-remain-outside-initial-reentry rule of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- readable restart exclusion boundaries
- lower ambiguity around deferred material
- continuity between package depth and package usability
- a stable base for later boundary hardening

---

## 2. Exclusion Principle

What may remain outside initial reentry should remain understandable in terms of:
- what can safely be deferred
- what is contextual rather than immediately essential
- how deferred recovery preserves trust
- how exclusion prevents restart overload

---

## 3. Required Rule

Initial-reentry exclusion should remain:
- explicit
- selective
- meaningful
- readable
- non-bloated

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- arbitrary exclusion of essential material
- deferred material chosen only by habit or memory
- exclusion logic preserved only in operator memory
- restart framing that hides what was intentionally deferred

---

## 5. Final Rule

A mature documentation system defines what may wait until later so restart can begin safely and clearly.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical what-may-remain-outside-initial-reentry rule until replaced by a stricter exclusion reference.
