# 06 CANONICAL AUDIT HISTORY SEPARATION v1

Status: active_canonical
Document Type: canonical
Authority Level: constitutional
Interpretation Priority: strict
Scope: separation of canonical law, audit closure, and continuity history
Rule: canon, audit, and history must remain separable so the platform does not confuse present law with closure context or past narrative

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the separation model among canonical, audit, and history documents.

It exists to preserve:
- cleaner self-reading behavior
- lower risk of interpretation drift
- readable authority boundaries
- a stable base for future governance memory

---

## 2. Separation Principle

The documentation universe should preserve at least three distinct reading surfaces:

- active canonical law / contract surface
- audit / closure surface
- continuity / historical surface

These surfaces may relate to each other, but they must not collapse into one flat authority layer.

---

## 3. Required Rule

When a document is created, its role should be clear:
- does it govern current behavior
- does it record completion/closure
- does it preserve history/continuity

That distinction should be visible both in the header and in the registry.

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- treating historical explanation as current law
- treating audit completion as the same thing as active contract
- mixing closure documents into constitutional interpretation without distinction
- relying on filename pattern alone to separate document roles

---

## 5. Final Rule

A mature platform separates what is true now from how it got there.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical canonical-audit-history separation model until replaced by a stricter documentation-governance reference.
