# FULL PLATFORM VALIDATION DISCIPLINE v1

Status: active canonical full-platform validation discipline
Scope: whole-platform validation as ongoing engineering law
Rule: full-platform validation must remain mandatory discipline rather than occasional enthusiasm

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the discipline of full-platform validation.

It exists to preserve:
- whole-system integrity checking
- detection of cross-layer regressions
- resistance to false confidence from partial checks only

---

## 2. Full-Platform Principle

A large platform must periodically validate itself as a whole.

Partial checks are necessary.
Whole-platform checks are also necessary.

The project must preserve the distinction.

---

## 3. Required Rule

Full-platform validation should remain:
- explicit
- repeatable
- expected
- documented
- part of normal engineering discipline

It must not survive only as a heroic manual habit.

---

## 4. Why This Matters

Full-platform validation exists to detect:
- cross-layer drift
- hidden integration regressions
- slow structural inconsistency
- runtime or parallel execution issues not visible in narrow checks

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- replacing platform integrity with local convenience only
- calling the platform healthy forever because one subsystem is green
- letting whole-platform validation disappear from engineering rhythm

---

## 6. Final Rule

A serious platform validates the whole platform, not only the nearest file.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical full-platform validation discipline until replaced by a stricter integrity-validation reference.
