# MULTI AGENT ROLE BOUNDARY BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical multi-agent-role-boundary baseline
Scope: deeper role-boundary understanding for multi-agent behavior across the platform
Rule: multi-agent role boundaries must remain explicit so coordination depth does not collapse into ambiguous authority

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the multi-agent-role-boundary baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- explicit role separation in deeper multi-agent contexts
- readable distinction between role, capability, and authority
- bounded coordination semantics
- a stable base for later concrete multi-agent governance families

---

## 2. Boundary Principle

A multi-agent role boundary should remain understandable in terms of:
- what a role is for
- what capability surface it may access
- what it must not silently assume
- how it differs from neighboring roles
- how it remains subordinate to governance and approval

Role depth does not justify authority inflation.

---

## 3. Boundary Intent

Role-boundary thinking should help answer:
- what role is acting
- what that role may do
- what coordination meaning applies
- what authority still remains outside the role itself

---

## 4. Required Rule

Multi-agent role-boundary thinking should remain:
- explicit
- capability-aware
- bounded
- governance-aware
- consistent with governed-action and approval discipline

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- role boundaries dissolving into vague swarm language
- capability growth with no readable role limit
- coordination complexity treated as authority by itself
- hidden role overlap that obscures governance

---

## 6. Final Rule

A mature multi-agent platform keeps role boundaries explicit even as coordination grows deeper.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical multi-agent-role-boundary baseline until replaced by a stricter multi-agent role-boundary reference.
