# GOVERNED SWARM EXPANSION BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical governed-swarm-expansion baseline
Scope: deeper baseline for bounded expansion of swarm behavior under governance and approval discipline
Rule: swarm expansion must remain governed so deeper collaboration does not become uncontrolled authority growth

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the governed-swarm-expansion baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- readable expansion logic for deeper swarm behavior
- bounded relation between growth and governance
- continuity between multi-agent coordination and approval discipline
- a stable base for later concrete governed-expansion procedures

---

## 2. Expansion Principle

Governed swarm expansion should remain understandable in terms of:
- what new coordination surface is being introduced
- what roles and capabilities are expanding
- what governance constraints continue to apply
- what approval thresholds remain necessary
- what legitimacy limits remain unchanged despite greater complexity

Expansion does not erase governance.

---

## 3. Expansion Intent

Governed-expansion thinking should help answer:
- what swarm growth is occurring
- how that growth remains bounded
- what approval and legitimacy rules still govern it
- how expansion stays subordinate to platform truth and control discipline

---

## 4. Required Rule

Governed swarm expansion thinking should remain:
- explicit
- bounded
- approval-aware
- legitimacy-aware
- consistent with governed-action principles

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- swarm growth treated as self-justifying
- deeper coordination presented as automatic authority
- governance fading as swarm complexity increases
- expansion with no readable boundedness model

---

## 6. Final Rule

A mature multi-agent platform expands under governance, not around it.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical governed-swarm-expansion baseline until replaced by a stricter governed swarm reference.
