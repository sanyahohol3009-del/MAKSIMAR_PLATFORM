# ADVANCED MODULE ADMISSION BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical advanced-module-admission baseline
Scope: deeper admission thinking for advanced modules entering governed multi-agent contexts
Rule: advanced-module admission must remain explicit so deeper expansion does not become silent legitimacy creep

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the advanced-module-admission baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- explicit admission thinking for advanced modules
- readable distinction between existence, capability, and legitimacy
- bounded integration of advanced modules into governed contexts
- a stable base for later concrete admission and approval procedures

---

## 2. Admission Principle

Advanced-module admission should remain understandable in terms of:
- what module is entering a governed environment
- what role or capability surface it seeks
- what approval or legitimacy boundaries apply
- what it may support without redefining the platform root

Admission is not equivalent to unrestricted participation.

---

## 3. Admission Intent

Advanced-module admission thinking should help answer:
- what module is being admitted
- what context it is entering
- what constraints must apply
- why that admission remains governed and bounded

---

## 4. Required Rule

Advanced-module admission thinking should remain:
- explicit
- boundary-aware
- approval-aware
- governance-aware
- subordinate to platform legitimacy rules

---

## 5. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- advanced modules entering governed contexts by assumption only
- capability expansion with no readable admission boundary
- module novelty treated as legitimacy by itself
- silent root redefinition through advanced-module sprawl

---

## 6. Final Rule

A mature platform admits advanced modules deliberately, not by fascination or drift.

---

## 7. Status

This document is the active canonical advanced-module-admission baseline until replaced by a stricter admission reference.
