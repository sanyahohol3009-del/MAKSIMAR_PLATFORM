# ROLE AND COORDINATION INTERPRETATION v1

Status: active canonical role-and-coordination interpretation model
Scope: how agent roles and coordination behavior should be understood operationally
Rule: role and coordination interpretation must remain structured enough that multi-agent behavior is intelligible and bounded

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the role and coordination interpretation model of the platform.

It exists to preserve clarity about:
- how role differences should be understood
- what coordination changes mean
- why bounded multi-agent states should remain interpretable
- why coordination role must stay separate from platform authority

---

## 2. Interpretation Principle

Role-aware coordination should remain capable of distinguishing:
- stable role-based behavior
- bounded collaboration
- governed advanced-module interplay
- degraded or constrained coordination mode
- uncertainty or transition state where applicable

Coordination should make complexity manageable without collapsing it into confusion.

---

## 3. Required Rule

Role and coordination interpretation should remain explainable in terms of:
- role purpose
- capability limits
- coordination meaning
- continuity expectations
- non-authoritative relation to core platform legitimacy

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- role meaning disappearing into vague swarm language
- coordination changes with no interpretive model
- treating larger coordination as the only real or valid mode
- operator confusion caused by hidden coordination transitions

---

## 5. Final Rule

Role and coordination logic should help preserve stable meaning across changing multi-agent realities.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical role-and-coordination interpretation model until replaced by a stricter swarm-operations reference.
