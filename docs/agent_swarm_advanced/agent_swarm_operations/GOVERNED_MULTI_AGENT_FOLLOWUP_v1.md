# GOVERNED MULTI AGENT FOLLOWUP v1

Status: active canonical governed multi-agent followup orientation
Scope: how followup should remain governed after agent/swarm coordination produces results or state changes
Rule: multi-agent followup must remain governance-bounded, not drift into automatic authority from coordination itself

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the governed multi-agent followup orientation of the platform.

It exists to preserve clarity about:
- why coordination output alone is not enough
- how followup should remain governed
- why multi-agent reasoning must not become self-authorizing execution

---

## 2. Followup Principle

Multi-agent followup should not stop at “the swarm produced something.”

It should support understanding of:
- what role configuration was relevant
- what capability boundaries applied
- whether approval or review is still required
- what further inspection, validation, or governed next step is appropriate

---

## 3. Required Rule

Multi-agent followup should remain:
- bounded
- governance-aware
- approval-aware
- explainable
- distinct from authority to redefine platform truth or legitimacy

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- swarm output treated as automatic authority
- followup drifting into self-legitimizing execution
- coordination scale substituting for approval or governance
- confusion between bounded coordination and platform truth ownership

---

## 5. Final Rule

Multi-agent reasoning should help the platform move from coordination to governed understanding, not from complexity to uncontrolled authority.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical governed multi-agent followup orientation until replaced by a stricter multi-agent governance reference.
