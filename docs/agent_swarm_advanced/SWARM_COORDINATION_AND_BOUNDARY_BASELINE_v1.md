# SWARM COORDINATION AND BOUNDARY BASELINE v1

Status: active canonical swarm coordination/boundary baseline
Scope: how multi-agent coordination should remain bounded and structured
Rule: swarm coordination must remain explicit, role-aware, and governance-bounded so cooperation does not mutate into uncontrolled authority

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the current swarm coordination and boundary baseline of the platform.

It exists to preserve clarity about:
- how coordination differs from authority
- why multiple agents do not automatically create legitimacy
- how swarm thinking remains bounded
- why coordination must stay subordinate to platform truth and governance

---

## 2. Coordination Principle

Swarm coordination may support:
- division of roles
- distributed interpretation
- bounded collaboration
- structured task decomposition
- future advanced-module interplay

But coordination is not automatic permission to:
- redefine truth
- bypass approval
- collapse governance
- create a second hidden platform root

---

## 3. Required Rule

Swarm coordination should remain:
- explicit
- role-aware
- boundary-aware
- explainable
- subordinate to approval, trust, and action-legitimacy discipline

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- uncontrolled swarm behavior
- multi-agent coordination with no role boundaries
- swarm scale treated as authority by itself
- coordination paths that bypass platform governance

---

## 5. Final Rule

Coordination is useful when it remains bounded and governed, not when it becomes an excuse for uncontrolled complexity.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical swarm coordination/boundary baseline until replaced by a stricter swarm reference.
