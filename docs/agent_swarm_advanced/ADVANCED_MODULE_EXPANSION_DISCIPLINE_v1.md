# ADVANCED MODULE EXPANSION DISCIPLINE v1

Status: active canonical advanced-module expansion discipline
Scope: how advanced modules may expand without damaging foundational structure
Rule: advanced modules must remain modular, documented, and subordinate to foundational platform disciplines

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the expansion discipline for advanced modules in the platform.

It exists to preserve clarity about:
- how advanced modules may be added
- why advanced expansion must not damage core structure
- why novelty must remain subordinate to foundational integrity

---

## 2. Expansion Principle

Advanced modules may extend the platform,
but they must not:
- redefine foundational truth
- bypass governance
- collapse role boundaries
- become hidden compulsory roots

Advanced expansion should remain:
- modular
- documented
- bounded
- explainable
- validation-aware

---

## 3. Required Rule

Before an advanced module expands materially, the project should remain able to explain:
- where it sits
- what it depends on
- what it must not redefine
- what capability or role boundaries apply
- how its legitimacy remains governed

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- advanced-module sprawl with no boundary model
- hidden authority inflation through module count or complexity
- expansion that outruns documentation and governance
- advanced functionality treated as self-justifying

---

## 5. Final Rule

Advanced modules strengthen a platform only when they remain disciplined additions, not structural hijacks.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical advanced-module expansion discipline until replaced by a stricter advanced-module governance reference.
