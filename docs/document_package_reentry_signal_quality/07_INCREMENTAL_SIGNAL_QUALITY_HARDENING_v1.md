---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the incremental-signal-quality-hardening rule of the platform.

It exists to preserve:
- practical signal hardening
- lower retrofit overload
- continuity between signal ambition and real maintenance work
- a stable base for later tooling

---

## 2. Hardening Principle

Incremental signal quality hardening should remain understandable in terms of:
- what package family is hardened next
- why it is prioritized
- what minimum signal quality is expected
- how hardening grows without chaos

---

## 3. Required Rule

Incremental signal quality hardening should remain:
- explicit
- incremental
- canonical-first
- practical
- non-chaotic

---

## 4. What Is Forbidden

The following remain forbidden:
- pretending full signal hardening will happen instantly
- uncontrolled retrofit pressure
- random hardening order
- signal hardening with no readable sequencing

---

## 5. Final Rule

A mature documentation system hardens reentry signal quality in a controlled sequence, not through one blind sweep.

---

## 6. Status

This document is the active canonical incremental-signal-quality-hardening rule until replaced by a stricter hardening reference.
